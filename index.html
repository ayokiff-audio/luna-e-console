About this console

The SSL sound comes from your installed Universal Audio plugins in normal insert slots. This Add On provides a shared control surface; it does not register a new LUNA console Extension.

The native companion adds original console-inspired harmonics and low-level peer leakage. It is not a measured SSL circuit model. Crosstalk is off initially; enabled track-role instances share audio with up to two nearest seed neighbors in the same console group. Buses and the master can receive track leakage without sending audio upstream. Up to 64 enabled track-role instances can share audio in one host process; additional instances still add color.

The companion reports 4096 samples of latency. Use LUNA’s delay compensation and leave it out of low-latency recording paths. Crosstalk requires instances in the same host process; offline render behavior must be checked in LUNA. The experimental saturation does not use anti-alias oversampling.

Controls follow LUNA, support automation and undo, and reset to the plugin’s own defaults on double-click.
