OFFLINEOpen LUNA and a session

This console controls your installed SSL plugins in LUNA. Drag knobs vertically; double-click a knob or fader to restore its plugin default. Click a channel name to select it, then use Setup to add SSL and its analog companion into empty inserts.

Crosstalk all on connects every loaded companion to the shared group and enables audio interaction, including buses and the master. Crosstalk all off disables it everywhere. You can still exclude an individual strip with its own switch. Tracks publish and receive; buses and the master receive without publishing. Each companion reports 4096 samples of latency.

PREVIEW uses simulated controls and makes no audio. In LIVE mode, meters show only values received from LUNA. This independent console is not a native LUNA console Extension or a measured SSL circuit model.
